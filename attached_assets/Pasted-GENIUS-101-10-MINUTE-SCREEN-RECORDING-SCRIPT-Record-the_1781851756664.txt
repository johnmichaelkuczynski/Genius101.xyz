GENIUS 101 — 10-MINUTE SCREEN RECORDING SCRIPT
Record the live app. Open and run every function start to finish. Let every generation
stream to completion on screen. Let the audit/database-activity panel show on every
generation so the viewer sees real corpus grounding, not invented text. No intro card,
no music, no captions over video, no fades. Just the app being used.

WHERE A FUNCTION TAKES TEXT INPUT, USE THE GIVEN INPUT VERBATIM SO REAL OUTPUT APPEARS.

----------------------------------------------------------------
0:00–0:40  SIDEBAR + MAIN CHAT (Figure Chat)
- Scroll the full philosopher sidebar so all 50+ thinkers pass by (Adler...Freud...Galileo).
- Type "Freud" in search to show search works. Click Freud.
- In chat, type the input below and send. LET THE AUDIT PANEL SHOW the positions/quotes/
  text_chunks queries firing, then let the streamed answer finish.
  INPUT: "Did Libet's experiment prove we lack free will? The subject is conscious of his
  intention only after it has long been in the making. He always feels as though he just
  came up with the decision and freely chose to move his finger. But the readiness
  potential was already underway. So the conscious event is an awareness of the decision,
  not the decision itself."

0:40–1:15  THINKING PANEL + DATABASE GROUNDING
- Keep the answer from streaming. Open/point to the audit panel and the thinking panel so
  the viewer sees actual quotes being pulled from the corpus during the response.
- Scroll the finished answer so its grounded quotes are visible.

1:15–2:15  PAPER WRITER
- Open Paper Writer. Enter the topic below, set length to 1500, click Generate.
- Show the audit panel gathering materials, then the paper streaming. Scroll it. Click
  the download/copy control at the end.
  INPUT: "A constructive response to the Libet Experiment. The experiment has been
  replicated many times with the same results. The constructive move is to accept the data
  and model it, not to insist it must be spurious. Standing intentions cannot be
  constituents of consciousness. Consciousness approves or vetoes decisions already made."

2:15–3:30  THE 6-STAGE WORKFLOW (the centerpiece — show ALL of it)
- Launch the workflow. Show each stage produce its artifact and that each is DOWNLOADABLE:
  STAGE 1 Gather Materials (positions, quotes, text_chunks listed)
  STAGE 2 Outline
  STAGE 3 Questions
  STAGE 4 Blueprint
  STAGE 5 Short Version
  STAGE 6 Final
- Mid-run, click COURSE-CORRECT, type a one-line correction, resume.
- Then on a later stage click HALT to show the emergency stop works.
- Open the Database Activity popup so live DB queries stream alongside the main output.

3:30–4:30  MODEL BUILDER
- Open Model Builder. Show both modes exist: toggle FORMAL (Domain/Interpretation/Axioms/
  Theorems) and INFORMAL (conceptual reinterpretation). Toggle "Model Entire Text."
- Paste the input below, click Generate, let the model stream into the right pane, download.
  INPUT: "If a stone became conscious while falling it would think it fell of its own free
  will. Find an interpretation on which the claim that conscious choices are awarenesses of
  prior decisions comes out true. Map W onto an after-the-fact approval signal. Map the
  readiness potential onto the operative cause."

4:30–5:30  DIALOGUE CREATOR
- Open Dialogue Creator. Set two thinkers (e.g., Freud and Libet), enter the topic, generate.
- Let the dialogue stream, scroll it, download.
  INPUT: "Are our conscious decisions really ours to make, or only ours to behold?"

5:30–6:30  INTERVIEW CREATOR

- Let the interview stream, scroll, download.
  INPUT: "Press the difference between asking what the Libet experiment shows and asking

6:30–7:30  DEBATE CREATOR
- Open Debate Creator. Stage Libet vs. Mele on "Is W the operative intention?", generate.

  INPUT: "Resolved: the readiness potential, not the conscious event W, is the operative

8:15–8:50  COMPARE THINKERS (header button)
